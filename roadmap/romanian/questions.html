Lab Topics

    Questions and Possible Answers

    This lab focuses on short Romanian questions and possible answers built from the vocabulary and simple sentence patterns you already learned. Each question has two possible answers so you can practice both asking and replying in realistic mini-exchanges.

    The lesson is organized into five categories with three subcategories each. Every subcategory contains three questions, and every question includes two possible answers. Audio is available for the question and for each answer option.
