Discover Romanian

Welcome to the Romanian roadmap. This track is designed for English speakers who want to learn Romanian step by step. You will build reading, listening, speaking, and practical conversation skills through guided labs.

Scope: This roadmap is English-based and beginner-friendly. Use each lab in order. Read the lesson, watch the media, and practice the exercises. By the end of the track, you should understand everyday Romanian and handle basic conversations with confidence.
