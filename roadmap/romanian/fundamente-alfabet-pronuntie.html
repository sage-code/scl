    English Description

    • Structure: Romanian alphabet has 31 letters based on the Latin script, including 5 specific diacritic characters: (Ă, Â, Î, Ș, Ț) with lowercase: (ă, â, î, ș, ț).
    • Phonetic Consistency: Operates as a nearly transparent orthography where graphemes map directly to single phonemes. That means Romanian text is easy to read.
    • Vowel Inventory: Features 7 vowels (A, E, I, O, U, Ă, Â/Î), where Ă, Â, and Î represent central vowel sounds unique to the language. Pay attention to these letters in the video.
    • Consonant System: Includes standard Latin consonants alongside Ș ([ʃ] as in "shoe") and Ț ([ts] as in "cats").
    • Inheritance: Evolved directly from the Latin alphabet with historical Slavic and regional phonological adaptations.
