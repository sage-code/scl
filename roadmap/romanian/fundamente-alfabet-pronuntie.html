    English Description

    • Structure: Romanian alphabet has 31 letters based on the Latin script, including 5 specific diacritic characters: (Ă, Â, Î, Ș, Ț) with lowercase: (ă, â, î, ș, ț).
    • Phonetic Consistency: Operates as a nearly transparent orthography where graphemes map directly to single phonemes. That means Romanian text is easy to read.
    • Vowel Inventory: Features 7 vowels (A, E, I, O, U, Ă, Â/Î), where Ă, Â, and Î represent central vowel sounds unique to the language. Pay attention to these letters in the video.
    • Consonant System: Includes standard Latin consonants alongside Ș ([ʃ] as in "shoe") and Ț ([ts] as in "cats").
    • Inheritance: Evolved directly from the Latin alphabet with historical Slavic and regional phonological adaptations.

    Digraphs and Trigraphs

    Next video introduce you to Romanian groups of letters. Special group of letters are pronounced like single sound. Watch the video before you study the tables provided below to understand digraphs and trigraphs. Watch the video:

    In Romanian orthography, digraphs and trigraphs are sequences of two or three letters that represent a single unique phoneme (speech sound) rather than the individual sounds of the separate letters.

    1. Digraphs (Digrame)

    These combinations dictate the pronunciation of consonants (specifically soft/palatalized variants of c and g) depending on the vowels that follow them:

    Digraph Phonetic Value English Approximation Example
    Ce / Ci /t͡ʃ/ ch as in "cheese" / "chin" "ceas" (watch), "ciocan" (hammer)
    Ge / Gi /d͡ʒ/ j as in "jump" / "gin" "geam" (window), "girafă" (giraffe)

    2. Trigraphs (Trigrame)

    Trigraphs consist of three letters functioning together as a single structural unit—typically combinations of the hard-stop digraphs preceding an additional vowel glide in specific morphological contexts.

    Digraph Phonetic Value English Approximation Example
    Che / Chi /kʲ/ Hard k followed by a front vowel glide "cheie" (key), "chip" (face)
    Ghe / Ghi /ɡʲ/ Hard g as in "get" before front vowels "gheată" (boot), "ghiocel" (snowdrop)

    Diphthongs and Triphthongs

    A specific rule in Romanian allows up to three vowels to cluster within a single syllable, creating distinct gliding sounds that differ significantly from individual vowel pronunciations. Understanding these phonetic rules, is allowing you to master accurate pronunciation effortlessly.
