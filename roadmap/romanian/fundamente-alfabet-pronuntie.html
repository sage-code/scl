    Alfabetul Român și Pronunție

    Alfabetul Românesc este ușor de învâțat cu video. Urmatorul tabel ne aratâ toate literele alfabetului Românesc și transcrierea internaționalâ IPA. Fără expplicații e mai greu. Oferim consultanță pe Discord pentru cei care doresc. Să începem:
    Caps Lowercase IPA English Approximation
    Aa/a/Like the a in father; open and clear, not clipped.
    Ăă/ə/Like the unstressed u in but; short central vowel.
    Ââ/ɨ/No exact English match; a central, unrounded vowel produced with the tongue higher in the mouth.
    Bb/b/Like the b in bed; voiced and direct.
    Cc/k/ or /tʃ/Like c in cat before a/o/u, and like ch in chin before e/i.
    Dd/d/Like the d in dog; clean voiced stop.
    Ee/e/Like the e in bed, but usually a bit steadier and more closed.
    Ff/f/Like the f in fan; same English friction.
    Gg/ɡ/ or /dʒ/Like g in go before a/o/u, and like g in gem before e/i.
    Hh/h/Like the h in hat; simple breathy sound.
    Ii/i/ or /j/Like the i in machine as a vowel, or like y in yes as a glide.
    Îî/ɨ/Same sound family as â; a central Romanian vowel with no direct English equivalent.
    Jj/ʒ/Like the s in measure or vision; voiced and smooth.
    Kk/k/Like the k in kite; mainly in loanwords.
    Ll/l/Like the l in leg; clear, not darkened.
    Mm/m/Like the m in map; nasal and voiced.
    Nn/n/Like the n in net; nasal and direct.
    Oo/o/Like the o in more, but usually shorter and cleaner.
    Pp/p/Like the p in pen; unvoiced and crisp.
    Rr/r/Rolled or tapped r, closer to Spanish than English.
    Ss/s/Like the s in sit; unvoiced hissing sound.
    Șș/ʃ/Like the sh in shoe; Romanian uses the diacritic to mark this sound.
    Tt/t/Like the t in top; short and unvoiced.
    Țț/ts/Like the ts in cats; a clear affricate, not a plain English t.
    Uu/u/Like the oo in boot; rounded and steady.
    Vv/v/Like the v in van; voiced and familiar.
    Ww/w/Used mostly in loanwords; usually like English w in wind.
    Xx/ks/ or /ɡz/Like x in box or x in exact, depending on position.
    Yy/j/ or /i/Mostly appears in loanwords; often sounds like y in yes.
    Zz/z/Like the z in zoo; voiced and sharp.

    English Description

    • Structure: Romanian alphabet has 31 letters based on the Latin script, including 5 specific diacritic characters: (Ă, Â, Î, Ș, Ț) with lowercase: (ă, â, î, ș, ț).
    • Phonetic Consistency: Operates as a nearly transparent orthography where graphemes map directly to single phonemes. That means Romanian text is easy to read.
    • Vowel Inventory: Features 7 vowels (A, E, I, O, U, Ă, Â/Î), where Ă, Â, and Î represent central vowel sounds unique to the language. Pay attention to these letters in the video.
    • Consonant System: Includes standard Latin consonants alongside Ș ([ʃ] as in "shoe") and Ț ([ts] as in "cats").
    • Inheritance: Evolved directly from the Latin alphabet with historical Slavic and regional phonological adaptations.
