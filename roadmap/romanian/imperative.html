    Imperative Statements

    Imperative statements give commands, instructions, requests, and warnings. In Romanian, the imperative is common in daily speech because people use it to guide actions in the home, classroom, city, workplace, and emergency situations. This lab practices short, direct imperative sentences built around vocabulary you already learned.

    Each table below contains simple imperative sentences with English translations. The sentences are numbered for drill practice, and every row has audio so you can hear the natural command form.
