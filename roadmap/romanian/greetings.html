    Greetings and Common Formulas

    This lab prepares you for real first-contact conversations with native speakers. You will learn how to choose the right formula based on context, relationship, and formality level, then apply everything in short, verifiable dialogs.

    Why Greetings Matter

    Real Context

    In Romanian, the first 3-5 seconds of a conversation set the social tone. An unsuitable greeting is not only a vocabulary issue but also a register issue: it may sound too distant, too familiar, or disrespectful.

    Formal vs Informal Register

    Core rule: start formal when you do not know the person, then move to informal only if the other person clearly signals it. Address formulas are closely tied to pronouns: dumneavoastră (formal) versus tu (informal).

    Micro-Dialog Structure

    A complete minimal exchange has three parts: greeting, short social question, and polite closing. Model: Salut + Ce mai faceți? + Mulțumesc, o zi bună!.

    Essential Formulas

    Casual Greetings

    Casual Formula Context
    Salut friends, close colleagues
    Bună daily informal situations
    Ceao very close friends

    Polite Greetings

    Polite Formula Context
    Bună dimineața morning, formal or neutral
    Bună ziua daytime, formal or neutral
    Bună seara evening, formal or neutral
    Noapte bună end of day, before sleep

    Conversation Closings

    Closing Formula Note / Description
    Pe curând Warm formula used when you expect to meet again soon.
    La revedere Standard polite form, suitable for formal and neutral contexts.
    Să fii sănătos Personal wish, more intimate; conveys care and respect.
    Noroc bun Rare/regional formula; may sound archaic, mainly used in traditional tone.
    Să auzim de bine Friendly closing with a positive future meaning.
    Să ne vedem sănătoși Affectionate wish, suitable when separation may be longer.
    Noapte bună Used late in the evening or before sleep.
    Adio și noroc Very strong; may suggest a final goodbye. Use carefully.

    Executable Examples

    You now have 3 simple examples. First read the dialog. Then watch the video. Repeat each sentence aloud and try to speak clearly and calmly.

    Example 1: interaction with an unknown person

    
Emil: Bună ziua!
-Ana: Bună ziua!
Emil: Ce mai faceți?
-Ana: Foarte bine, mulțumesc. Dumneavoastră?
Emil: Bine, mulțumesc. O zi frumoasă!
-Ana: La revedere!

    Example 2: colleague or friend

    
Emil: Ceao! Ce faci?
-Ana: Bine, tu?
Emil: Super. Mersi!
-Ana: A fost o plăcere. Ne vedem mai târziu.
Emil: Da bine. De-abia aștept!

    Example 3: service scenario

    
Emil: Bună ziua! Mă puteți ajuta, vă rog?
-Ana: Sigur. Cu ce vă pot ajuta?
Emil: Mulțumesc. Caut o carte pentru începători.
-Ana: Desigur. Poftiți această recomandare: Descoperă Limba Română
Emil: Interesant. Mulțumesc mult. La revedere!
-Ana: Cu plăcere. O zi bună!

    Pronunciation and Rhythm

    Challenging Sounds

    Diacritics are essential in Romanian. For example, the letter "a" can appear as "â" or "ă". These are not optional accents, but distinct letters with their own phonetic values that can change word meaning.

    Stress and Intonation

    Short social questions usually have moderate rising intonation at the end: Ce mai faceți?. Avoid exaggerated pitch rise, or the sentence may sound unnatural or hesitant.

    Interpretation Exercise

    Repeat the following key words 3 times in sequence:

    • Salut, Salut, Salut
    • Bună, bună, bună
    • Ceau, ceau, ceau
    • Noroc, Noroc, Noroc
    • Sănătate, Sănătos, Sănătoși

    Common Mistakes

    False Friends

    Romanian has words that look familiar to English speakers but have different meanings. For example, "hai" means "come on". Using it as a direct greeting can sound odd and may be misunderstood.

    Confusion Between tu and dumneavoastră

    Typical error: mixing both in one sentence, for example Ce faci dumneavoastră?. Choose one register per interaction.

    Literal Translation from English

    How do you do? should not be translated mechanically. In Romanian, natural greeting forms include Bună ziua, Ce mai faceți?, or Îmi pare bine, depending on context.

    Politeness Omissions

    Without scuze, vă rog, or mulțumesc, a message may sound imperative. Especially at the beginning, intentionally use polite formulas until the register feels natural.
