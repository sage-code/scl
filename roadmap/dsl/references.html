    How to Choose & Next Steps

    Follow the problem, not the hype:

    • Bare-metal firmware, boot code, or tiny footprints — start with Forth, then FASM when you need byte-exact control.
    • Digital hardware or FPGAs — learn Verilog (and HDLBits for practice).
    • Building compilers or language runtimes — study LLVM IR; Godbolt makes it visible in seconds.
    • Custom GPU kernels — write them in Triton before reaching for CUDA.
    • AI infrastructure and inference serving — evaluate Mojo; for broader scientific computing, Julia.
    • Statistics, modeling, and reporting — R for statistics, MATLAB/Octave for matrices, Wolfram for symbolic work, Stan for Bayesian inference.
    • Logic and reasoning — Prolog for interactive knowledge systems, Datalog for data-scale rules, Clingo for combinatorial search, MiniZinc for constraint optimization.
