Ada Roadmap

Ada is a structured, statically-typed, imperative, and object-oriented high-level programming language, extended from Pascal and other languages. It has built-in language support for design by contract, extremely strong typing, explicit concurrency, tasking, synchronous message passing, protected objects, and non-determinism.
