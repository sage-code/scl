English Roadmap

Learn English from scratch without translation. This lab uses a Bootstrap-based static study system with guided reading, embedded videos, and practical checkpoints that move from survival English to engineering communication.

No Translation Path

Train your brain to connect sound, image, and meaning directly in English instead of converting everything through another language.

Static Content

Every topic is written as static study material so it loads fast, stays readable, and remains available as a durable reference.

Embedded Videos

Use the video section to reinforce listening, pronunciation, grammar, and engineering vocabulary with guided visual practice.

English is a high-value global language for study, work, software, research, and collaboration. This roadmap starts with sound and meaning, then builds grammar accuracy, and ends with practical English for technical documents, issue tracking, meetings, and presentations.
