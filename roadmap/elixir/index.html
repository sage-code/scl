Elixir Roadmap

Elixir is a functional language that runs on the Erlang VM (BEAM) and is built for concurrent, fault-tolerant, distributed systems. Immutable data, lightweight processes with isolated heaps, and the OTP supervision model let a single codebase scale from one laptop to a global cluster. This track starts with why you would choose Elixir over Python or Rust, then deep-dives from first expressions to production OTP, Ecto, Phoenix, and performance work.

Scope: Study the labs in order. Phases 1–2 build language fluency, phase 3 adds metaprogramming and tooling, phase 4 is the OTP core, phase 5 covers the production ecosystem, phase 6 collects runnable demos and study projects, and phase 7 is the reference shelf.
