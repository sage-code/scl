    Next-steps map. Finished the track? Re-read your favourite lesson against the official source that inspired it — the gfortran docs for intrinsics, the Quickstart for structure, the OpenMP/MPI specs for parallelism — then pick one of the study projects and extend it until it breaks. That breaking-and-fixing loop, more than any single page here, is what turns a tutorial into an engineering skill.
    Keeping references current. Documentation links move as projects restructure their sites. When a link here fails, the resource still exists — search the project name on the fortran-lang site or the Discourse, which indexes the whole ecosystem and is the most reliable entry point of all.
