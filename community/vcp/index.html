What the credential certifies

The VCP credential is based on trust and true data. Is not just a badge it is an authentication ID credential. We verify your social presence to make sure you are not a machine, a spam agent or a scam artist. You must be who you say you are, authentic professional and trustworthy human being.

Certificate of completion

A signed certificate is issued for the track you follow and is archived in the community certificate collection.

Public contributor profile

Your name, role, certification and year are listed in the community directory, with link to your profile page.

Mentor endorsement

Mentors can endorse your skills on LinkedIn. Sage-Code verified members get priority on GitHub work items.

Prerequisite path

Order matters here. First pick a roadmap, if you are here to study. Next you can join Discord, subscribe to our Substack newsletter and follow us on LinkedIN. No paid subscription or registration is required.

  1. Pick a roadmap. The broad route is the Software Engineering roadmap. The short tracks — HTML, CSS, JScript or Dart — are the fastest way to win your first certificate.
  2. Ask for a review. Report the roadmap you completed in the Discord server. A mentor will review your work and approve your application. We design certificate of completion with QR code that can be verified by anyone with a phone.

How to apply

Applying for VCP is a conversation, not a single form you have to fill in. There is no fee and no subscription. Make sure you have performed the learning and you have the required social presence on any of LinkedIN, GitHub, BlueSky, Reddit, X Twitter, Discord or Substack.

  1. Complete at least one roadmap, so there is work to review.
  2. Post your progress in discord, include the roadmap name.
  3. Send your profile data, a link to your social profile will do.

Add your own profile page

SCL is open source. Any software developer can publish a profile page with the list of projects they wish to promote. You can modify and publish your own profile in two ways:

  • Make a pull request for the SCL project adding your profile page. We review it and merge it in the main branch.
  • Send a direct message to @elucian on Discord, or to any other Sage-Code mentor, to have your projects listed on your profile page.

You can also become a Sage-Code Developer using a direct message to a mentor or to the founder, @elucian.

Support the Lab

Roadmaps, certificates and profiles are produced by volunteers. Supporting the Lab is optional and separate from applying for VCP status, and it never changes a review outcome. Your status is granted on merit not on your monetary contribution.

Subscription on Ko-Fi is optional and is already available on the first page. Every contribution is spent on the laboratory: domains, build infrastructure and development time for roadmaps and projects.
