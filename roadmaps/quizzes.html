Roadmap Quizzes

These quizzes measure how well you recall, apply, and reason about roadmap concepts. Set A checks foundations, Set B combines core techniques, and Set C tests engineering judgment. Use the results to identify gaps and guide further study. Each quiz allows two minutes per question.

Quiz terms

  • Each quiz allows two minutes per question. The round timer updates every five seconds and submits your current answers when time expires.
  • A score of at least 70% passes. Submitted quiz attempts are scored and saved to your roadmap session; the catalog also keeps a local status for quick display.
  • Passing all three quizzes (A, B, and C) for a roadmap with a confirmed account adds a roadmap certificate to your community profile.
  • After you take a quiz, we recommend ordering a certificate of completion from our Ko-fi shop. We will design the certificate and publish a digital image on your profile. Pass all three quizzes before claiming your certificate.
